01 · MOTIVATION

Why I chose this work

Local decisions deserve long-term thinking.

Across every level of government, communities are asking whether today's choices are transparent, financially responsible and sustainable over time. That question matters most where government is closest to home.

“How can residents see not only what the Town is building—but why, how it is funded and what happens next?”

02 · THE RESIDENT JOURNEY

The information exists—but the story is fragmented

One project can require many searches.

A resident may need to move among meeting calendars, agenda packets, budget archives and department documents—then interpret several PDFs—to understand one project's timeline, funding and impact.

This is an illustrative journey based on the structure of the Town's official financial archives and Town Board calendar. It describes an access challenge, not a lack of public records.

northcastleny.gov / resident search
ILLUSTRATIVE RESIDENT SEARCH
  1. 1Find the relevant Town Board meeting
  2. 2Open the agenda packet or minutes
  3. 3Locate the project inside a long PDF
  4. 4Search separately for costs and funding
  5. 5Reconstruct what changed over time
The resident knows the project name—but not which meeting, archive or department page contains the latest information.

04 · THE RESPONSE

From scattered records to one understandable story

That gap became the idea for STEWARD.

Simple Transparency & Education With Accessible Resident Data brings each project's purpose, timeline, funding, resident impact and source records into one consistent public framework.

Today’s search

  • Multiple pages and archives
  • Separate PDFs and meeting records
  • Difficult status comparisons
  • No single project narrative
→

With STEWARD

  • One project-centered view
  • Timeline, costs and funding together
  • Clear labels for estimates and approvals
  • Direct links to underlying records
05 · STEWARD
